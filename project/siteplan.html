Odyssey Fitness

Website Planning Document

Site Name

Odyssey Fitness

This name represents a full-service traditional gym where members begin a personal "odyssey" — a journey of transformation through strength training, cardio, and group fitness classes.

Site Purpose

Odyssey Fitness provides information about gym memberships, class schedules, and trainer profiles to help visitors choose the right fitness plan and start their fitness journey. The site also allows visitors to sign up for a free trial or membership consultation through a contact form.

Scenarios

  • What group fitness classes are offered this week, and at what times?
  • How much does a monthly membership cost, and what does it include?
  • How can I sign up for a free trial class?

Color Scheme

  • #1a1a1a (dark charcoal) — primary background, header, footer
  • #f5f5f5 (off-white) — text on dark backgrounds, alternate section backgrounds
  • #ff4d00 (vibrant orange) — accents: buttons, links, calls to action

Typography

  • Oswald — used for headings and class/plan names
  • Montserrat — used for body text, descriptions, and form labels
